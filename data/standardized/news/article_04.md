## GENERAL ADMISSIONS
###  What majors does VinUni offer?
VinUniversity has four Colleges and offers the following degree programs:
College of Business and Management:
  * Bachelor of Business Administration (4 years)


College of Health Sciences:
  * Medical Doctor (6 years)
  * Bachelor of Nursing (4 years)


College of Engineering and Computer Science:
  * Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (4 years)
  * Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering (4 years)
  * Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering (4 years)
  * Bachelor of Science in Data Science (4 years)


College of Arts and Sciences:
  * Bachelor of Arts and Sciences in Psychology
  * Bachelor of Arts and Sciences in Economics
  * Bachelor of Arts and Sciences in Multimedia


###  Who is eligible to apply for VinUni?
  * High school diploma or equivalent certificate (A Level/IB…)
  * English Proficiency: IELTS 6.5 or higher, with no skill below 6.0
  * GPA: 8.0 or higher (recommended – grades are just one evaluation factor)
  * AACC Standards (Aspiration, Ability, Academic, Creativity)


**OR:**
  * TOEFL iBT: Minimum 79 with at least 20 in writing, 18 in speaking, 15 in reading, and 15 in listening.
  * Pearson Test of English (Academic) – Minimum total score of 58 with no communication skill below 50.
  * Cambridge Advanced English (CAE) – Minimum 176 with no skill below 169 in any part.


Since 2026, VinUniversity requires all incoming students to have an IELTS score of 6.5 overall (with no individual band below 6.0) or an equivalent qualification. Therefore, the English test organized by VinUniversity and the English Pathway program will no longer be offered from the 2026 academic year.
**Note:**
For students applying to MD, additional requirements applied:
  * Vietnamese requirements (for international students):
    * **Upon enrollment** must demonstrate A2 proficiency in Vietnamese.
    * **Upon entering clinical phase** of study (Year 4) must achieve B2 proficiency in speaking and listening, and B1 in writing and reading.
    * Students needing to demonstrate proficiency will sit for Vietnamese language proficiency examination arranged in advance of start of semester.
  * Science requirements:
    * Students must complete Biology, Chemistry, and one additional science course (Math or Physics) in high school in order to be eligible for the MD program.


###  When does VinUni enroll students?
There are three admission rounds:
  * **Early Round:** October 15 – January 15
  * **Regular Round** : February 15 – May 15
  * **Rolling Round:** June 15 – August 15


###  If I submit my application early, will I receive any benefits?
The ‘privileges’ for successful applicants in the early admission period:
  * Low competition rate
  * Waiver of 2,000,000 VND application fee for students who attend the recruitment event
  * Abundant scholarship opportunities


###  How can I apply to VinUni?
The application process includes the following steps:
* **Step 1:** Prepare your documents
  * Personal information
  * 1-page CV (VinUni template)
  * Academic records: high school transcripts, English proficiency certificate, other academic certificates (if any)
  * Short essay
  * Extracurricular activities / achievementsAny other achievements, certificates, if applicable
  * Information of referees (teachers who know the applicant best)


* **Step 2:** Contact the Admission Office via **HOTLINE 18008189** for further guidance.

###  How long does it take to receive the admission results after submitting my application?
After one and a half months after submitting your application, you will receive the scholarship results, and after two months, you will receive the financial aid results.
###  Am I allowed to defer my admission result?
  * The admission result can be deferred for up to one academic year in case you have a valid reason. However, please note that you must submit a deferral request **at least 30 days** before the start of the first semester of the first year. The admissions council will review and approve the applicant’s deferral request.
  * Applicants are only **allowed to defer once**.
  * Deferred scholarship results are **conditional** – when the applicant reapplies to the university, the council will review and reassess the scholarship results (meaning the scholarship result may increase or decrease).
  * Financial aid results **cannot be deferred** as they are assessed based on the applicant’s financial circumstances at the time of the admissions application.


###  Can I indicate my intention to take the AP exam during the scholarship application process and provide the results later?
You do not need to provide predicted scores if you have not taken the exams yet. Instead, you only need to supplement your application with your complete GPA from high school. This is because your scholarship results depend not only on your grades but also on evaluations based on the criteria set by the AACC.
###  Will I be eligible for credit transfer if I have completed AP/IB courses?
– After enrollment, you can apply for credit exemption for equivalent courses in the program.
– The exemption evaluation depends on the degree of equivalence of the course, the results may be:
+ Fully exempted and recognized with a “”T-Transfer”” grade.
+ Conditionally exempted (for example: additional practical scores or achieving a passing score in the course exam may still be required) to ensure students have equivalent knowledge.
